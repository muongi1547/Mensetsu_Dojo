## A. Verified (official or major source)

### A1. What interviewers evaluate and how they judge Japanese (Task 2)
- **What interviewers watch most** (Mynavi company survey, 2027 graduates):
  - communication skills (コミュニケーション能力) 50.6%
  - fit with the workplace atmosphere 38.9%
  - brightness, smile and approachability 36.8%
  - enthusiasm to join (熱意) 33.6%
  - openness and growth potential (素直さ・伸びしろ) 24.9%
  - sincerity (誠実さ) 19.5%
  - cooperativeness (協調性) 15.2%
  - stress tolerance 12.2%
  - understanding of the company and industry 11.0%
  - word choice and attitude (言葉遣い・態度) 10.3%
  - explaining what you study 5.4%
  - technical knowledge 2.8%
  - [source: https://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/after_study_j/job/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/10/30/guide_2028_all.pdf p.4, citing マイナビ「2027年卒 企業新卒採用活動調査」 | confidence: high | 2026]
- **Qualities companies want from international students**, science and engineering: Japanese ability 51.5%, communication 48.5%, cooperativeness 35.9%, specialist knowledge 35.0%, basic academic ability 19.4%. JASSO says Japanese ability and communication ability matter most. [source: https://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/after_study_j/job/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/10/30/guide_2028_all.pdf p.2, citing キャリタス「外国人留学生／高度外国人材の採用に関する調査」 | confidence: high | survey 2025-12]
- **Level of Japanese required:** about 70% of companies want "business intermediate" or higher at the offer stage, and about 90% after joining. The science and engineering chart reads, offer stage then after joining: native-like 10.7→23.3%, business advanced 22.3→39.8%, business intermediate 32.0→24.3%, business basic 20.4→9.7%, daily conversation 12.6→2.9%. [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.8 | confidence: high for the 70%/90% summary; medium for per-level values, which I read from the chart text | 2025-12]
- **What each stage tests:** the ES tests writing. Interviews test listening (understanding explanations) and speaking (getting your own points across). [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.8 | confidence: high]
- **Listening matters more than accuracy:** not looking at the interviewer, or ignoring other candidates, is "日本語を間違えることよりも、マイナス評価になります". If a question is unclear, ask for it again. This shows you check instead of guessing. [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/guide/howto/foreign-manners03/ | confidence: high | 2023-04-03]
- **Keidanren survey:** communication was the #1 selection factor for 13 years in a row, and initiative (主体性) was #2. I found no newer edition. [source: https://www.keidanren.or.jp/journal/times/2016/1124_05.html | confidence: high | 2016-11-24]
- **Employer-side guidance (MHLW):** R&D roles can need less Japanese because technical terms are international. Employers should also check whether a candidate wants to return home later. [source: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/bunya/koyou/oshirase/dl/110224aa_0004.pdf | confidence: high | undated; file name suggests 2011]
- **Response to student AI use:** 36.2% of companies now change their interview questions (asking beyond the ES, deeper 深掘り) because students use generative AI. [source: https://career-research.mynavi.jp/reserch/20260722_112587/ | confidence: high | 2026-07-22]

### A2. Interview formats (Task 3)
- **Interview types** (usually two or more rounds):
  - Group discussion: 4–6 students, with roles of facilitator, note-taker, timekeeper and presenter. It is usually in the 1st round. It is cooperative, not a debate.
  - Group interview: 2–6 applicants, in the 1st or 2nd round. Prepare a self-PR of about 1 minute.
  - Individual interview: 1st, 2nd or final round. It starts from your ES or résumé, then goes deeper and checks your intent to join.
  - [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, pp.54–55 | confidence: high]
- **Differences between rounds:**
  - 一次: HR or young staff, about 30 minutes, checks basics and manners.
  - 二次: line managers, 30–60 minutes, checks fit, skills and values, and digs into your 一次 answers.
  - 最終: president or executives, 30–60 minutes, checks intent to join and agreement with the company's vision.
  - The article estimates pass rates of 30–50%, 30–50% and 50–80%. Keep answers consistent across rounds.
  - [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/guide/howto/174/ | confidence: medium-high | 2026-01-14]
- **Final-round questions:** final interviews ask whether this company is your first choice and which other companies you are considering. [source: https://tir-navicenter.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/english/student/syukatsu/no-13en | confidence: high | info as of 2020-01]
- **Technical interviews:** engineering staff run them, usually in later rounds, and the questions are specialised. [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/guide/step/science-interview/ | confidence: high | 2023-04-03]
- **Web interviews:**
  - Log in 5 minutes early.
  - Put the camera at eye level and look into it while speaking.
  - Speak louder than usual and make nods and gestures bigger.
  - If the connection drops, contact the company by chat or phone.
  - Leave only after the interviewer disconnects.
  - Check the background, lighting, microphone, and your account name and profile picture.
  - An HR comment warns that reading from notes is obvious.
  - [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, pp.3, 57–58 | confidence: high]
- **Recorded video interviews:** mostly used in the 1st round. You give 1–2 minute answers on self-PR, ガクチカ or 志望動機, and time management is judged. [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.57 | confidence: high]
- **In-person flow:**
  - Arrive 15 minutes early.
  - Knock 2–3 times and say 失礼します.
  - Give your school, faculty and name.
  - Sit only when invited, and use a 30° bow.
  - Thank them before leaving.
  - [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.56 | confidence: high]
- **Hiring calendar:**
  - The official rule is publicity from 1 March of the year before graduation, interviews from 1 June, and formal offers from 1 October. Contact with companies starts earlier, and year-round hiring (通年採用) is common.
  - For an April start, the visa status change should be filed by the end of January.
  - [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, pp.10–11, 59 | confidence: high]

### A4. Technical topics: verified definitions (Task 4)
- **Driving-automation levels** (JASO TP names):
  - 0: 運転自動化なし (no automation)
  - 1: 運転支援 (driver assistance)
  - 2: 部分運転自動化 (partial automation; the driver still handles object and event detection and response)
  - 3: 条件付運転自動化 (conditional automation)
  - 4: 高度運転自動化 (high automation)
  - 5: 完全運転自動化 (full automation, with no area limit)
  - From level 3, the system performs the entire driving task.
  - [source: https://www.city.sendai.jp/sesakukoho/gaiyo/shichoshitsu/kaiken/2025/10/documents/jidouuntenlevel.pdf | high | 2025-10-14]
- **MLIT vehicle names:** levels 1–2 運転支援車; level 3 条件付自動運転車（限定領域）; level 4 自動運転車（限定領域）; level 5 完全自動運転車. [source: https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/press/jidosha07_hh_000353.html | high | 2020-12-11]
- **ISO 26262 (機能安全, functional safety):** covers hazards from malfunctions in electrical and electronic systems. ASIL levels run A–D, with D the strictest, and are set from severity, exposure and controllability. [source: https://www.synopsys.com/ja-jp/glossary/what-is-asil.html | high]
- **ISO 21448 SOTIF (意図した機能の安全性):** covers hazards with no failure, from performance limits (性能限界), the environment, or misuse. It complements ISO 26262 and was published in June 2022. [source: https://www.pwc.com/jp/ja/knowledge/column/automotive-research-and-development/iso-sotif.html | high | 2022-07-06]
- **SDV:** the METI/MLIT モビリティDX戦略 (May 2024) defines it as a next-generation car whose functions are continuously updated through cloud communication, creating new value such as more advanced driving functions. The target is a 30% Japanese share of global SDV sales by 2030/2035. [source: https://www.webcg.net/articles/-/50313 | medium-high | 2024-05-31]
- **AUTOSAR (オートザー):** an industry partnership founded in 2003 that standardises ECU software architecture. [source: https://www.weblio.jp/content/autosar | high]
- **Model compression:**
  - 量子化 (quantization) lowers numeric precision, for example FP32 to INT8, to make models smaller and faster.
  - 枝刈り (pruning) removes low-importance parts.
  - 知識蒸留 (knowledge distillation) transfers knowledge from a large model to a small one.
  - [source: https://www.tensorflow.org/lite/performance/model_optimization?hl=ja ; https://www.ultralytics.com/ja/blog/pruning-and-quantization-in-computer-vision-a-quick-guide | medium-high]
- **In-vehicle generative AI examples:** Mercedes' MBUX assistant uses an LLM; VW added ChatGPT through Cerence; GM plans a Gemini-based assistant in late 2026. [source: https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2401/18/news089.html ; https://www.marklines.com/ja/news/348702 | medium]
- **FPT Automotive job postings** (FPT Automotive is a division of FPT Japan Holdings; both postings are mid-career):
  - AI Engineer (HMI/BrSE): bridges customers and offshore teams, reviews SRS, builds PoCs, and validates AI output. Requires Python, ML/LLM basics, prompt engineering and JLPT N2 or higher. RAG, LangChain and ASPICE are preferred. [source: https://hrmos.co/pages/fpt-japan/jobs/FSJ_FA_AIEN_RD1 | high | accessed 2026-11-24]
  - AUTOSAR engineer: requires AUTOSAR Classic/Adaptive and ISO 26262 knowledge. [source: https://hrmos.co/pages/fpt-japan/jobs/FSJ_Automotive_Autosar_RD1 | high | accessed 2026-11-24]
- **Acronym readings confirmed by a source:**
  - ADAS エーダス [source: https://jpn.nec.com/nsi/ss/mobility/column/11/ | medium]
  - LiDAR ライダー [source: https://jidounten-lab.com/y_2520 | medium]
  - ONNX オニキス, CUDA クーダ, NVIDIA エヌビディア [source: https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2608/17/news020_2.html | high | 2026-08-17]

### A5. Common mistakes and what impresses (Task 5)
- **Manners mistakes:** entering without knocking, sitting before you are invited, leaving abruptly, poor eye contact, and dominating the group discussion. [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/guide/howto/foreign-manners03/ | high | 2023-04-03]
- **Word choice:**
  - Use 私 or わたくし, not 僕, 自分 or 俺.
  - Say 御社 when speaking and write 貴社.
  - Use 承知しました or かしこまりました instead of 了解しました.
  - Use 申し訳ございません instead of ごめんなさい.
  - [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.34 | high]
- **Content mistakes:** having no 逆質問 (rated lowest), giving memorised model answers, exaggerating (risky because interviewers dig in), and long preambles. Taking a moment to think is acceptable. [source: https://job.mynavi.jp/conts/2027/sci/rikeisemi/006.html | high]
- **Weak 逆質問:** questions the website already answers, repeats of what was just explained, and questions only about pay, holidays or overtime. [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/guide/howto/035/ | high | 2026-02-25]
- **Impresses:** answers that match your ES, polite careful answers (they signal sincerity), and actually understanding the question before answering in Japanese. [source: JASSO 2028 URL above, p.58 | high]

## B. Reported or anecdotal
- **FPT Japan, 2027 graduates, technical roles** (5 member posts):
  - Flow: ES → web test (verbal, non-verbal, personality) → 1st → 2nd → final interview. No group discussion was reported.
  - Reported topics: motivation, ガクチカ, strengths and weaknesses; why FPT rather than other IT firms; the hardest part of research or activities; why FPT Japan Holdings at the final round.
  - [source: https://job.career-tasu.jp/corp/00105422/counterplan/ | medium-low | undated]
- **DENSO technical track, 2027 graduates:** about 1 hour online, with about 20 minutes on research and then ES deep-dives. HR asked about motives, and engineers asked about research and ガクチカ. Honesty about technology and logical consistency were stressed. [source: https://gaishishukatsu.com/selection_reports/43333 | low-medium | 2026]
- **paiza (engineer recruiting media):**
  - Master's students explain their research in detail; prepare 10-, 5-, 3- and 1-minute versions.
  - Expect CS fundamentals such as sorting and search, and simple coding problems.
  - Expect "why this tool" questions; a vague "no real reason" is weak.
  - Expect problem-solving scenarios.
  - [source: https://paiza.jp/student/advice/student_interview_engineer | medium | undated]
- **ML interview topics (KOTORA):**
  - Topics: supervised vs unsupervised learning, 過学習 causes and prevention, train/validation/test roles, choosing 評価指標, features and preprocessing, reasons for model choice, why accuracy drops in production, data shortage/missing values/bias, priorities for improvement, reproducibility/explainability/fairness, and monitoring.
  - New graduates should show fundamentals and learning ability.
  - Admit gaps and give a learning plan; don't overstate your role.
  - [source: https://www.kotora.jp/c/199276-2/ | medium | 2024-10-09, updated 2026-10-09]
- **Akita University (international student's slides):**
  - Give reasons to work in Japan, not reasons to live there, and prepare three of them.
  - Expect "why do you think so?" asked about three times.
  - Record yourself to remove fillers (なんか, まあ, えっと, あの).
  - Avoid answers that would fit any company.
  - [source: https://www.akita-u.ac.jp/honbu/work/pdf/2020_maf01.pdf | medium | 2020]
- **One international student (Mynavi 国際派就職):** a self-introduction that was too long hurt; cutting it to 1–2 points helped. Overly polite phrasing backfired. Prepare why a company should hire you as a foreigner. [source: https://job.mynavi.jp/conts/global/2028/studyinjapan/interview/04/ | medium]
- **Speaking pace (mid-career site):** a 1-minute script is about 300 characters; speech runs about 350 characters per minute; each filler costs about 1 second. [source: https://mynavi-agent.jp/knowhow/pr/one-minute | medium]
- **Relocation (転勤):**
  - An HR columnist says to answer yes, with a forward-looking reason, mention that your family supports you, and raise constraints only after an offer. [source: https://news.mynavi.jp/article/dowell2021-16/ | medium | 2022-07-12]
  - Indeed says to be honest if you cannot relocate. [source: https://jp.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/geographical-preference | medium]
- **Team role:** describe the situation → how you saw your own role and acted → what you learned. [source: https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/osaka-young/content/contents/NO.48.pdf | medium; seen in search snippet only]
- **Stress coping:** the question tests self-management. Give healthy coping methods plus how you deal with the root cause; avoid binge eating or late-night gaming. [source: https://biz.moneyforward.com/payroll/basic/108241/ | medium | 2026-03-31]
- **Group discussion frequency:** 56.4% of 195 students had taken part in at least one group discussion. [source: https://reashu.com/research/group-discussion/ | low | 2025-11]
- **Vietnamese speakers (optional):**
  - Vietnamese has no vowel-length or geminate contrast. Learners often misjudge 長音 and 促音 (for example, hearing low short vowels as long). [source: https://js.vnu.edu.vn/FS/article/view/1069/1037 | medium | 2015]
  - Vietnamese career media flag keigo pitfalls: the ウチ/ソト rule, overusing させていただく, and 御社/貴社/弊社. [source: https://www.careerlink.vn/cam-nang-viec-lam/tu-van-nghe-nghiep/kinh-ngu-tieng-nhat-keigo-trong-cong-viec | low-medium]

## E. Suggestions for the practice system (my recommendations, not sourced facts)
- **Timer targets:** self-introduction 60 seconds; self-PR and ガクチカ 60–90 seconds; research in 1-, 3- and 5-minute versions.
- **Follow-up drill:** practise chains of three "why" questions after each answer.
- **Asking again:** teach phrases such as 恐れ入りますが、もう一度おっしゃっていただけますか.
- **Buying time:** teach phrases such as 少し考えてもよろしいでしょうか.
- **FPT Prep:** give LLM/RAG and BrSE-style communication extra weight, since FPT Automotive's AI posting focuses on them.

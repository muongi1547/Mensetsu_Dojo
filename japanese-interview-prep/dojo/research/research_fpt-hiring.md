# FPT Japan / FPT Automotive: how AI and automotive engineers are hired (checked 2026-11-24)

**How to read the sources.** FPT Japan Holdings (FPTジャパンホールディングス) runs one hiring portal (HRMOS) for all its Japan companies. Most engineers are employed by FPT Software Japan (FPTソフトウェアジャパン). Its automotive division is called FA (FPTオートモティブジャパン事業本部), and its job titles say "FPT Automotive". FPT Automotive is also the name of FPT's global automotive subsidiary. FCJ means FPT Consulting Japan. [source: https://hrmos.co/pages/fpt-japan/jobs/FSJ_Automotive_SE_RD1 | confidence: high | read 2026-11-24]

### 3. What FPT Japan says it values
- Japan Persona: One team (understand diverse colleagues and work for the team), Challenge (take on hard or new work, involve others, finish it), Lifelong Learning (keep learning and adapt to change). New-grad criteria: challenging and proactive, results-oriented, keeps learning, interest or skill in IT. [source: https://recruit.fpt-software.jp/newgraduates/ | confidence: high]
- Chief HR Officer: language skill must go beyond conversation. Staff should understand culture and business practice and make proposals themselves. The company values challenge and an improvement mindset, and staff are encouraged to ask customers what is new in each project rather than repeat what was done before. [source: https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/906705 | confidence: medium (sponsored content) | 2025-10-10]
- An article featuring the president stresses people who can contribute immediately, with business understanding and AI skills. It gives an FY2025 plan to add about 1,000 staff, about one third of them domestic new graduates. This does not match the 59 new-grad hires above, and the sources do not explain the gap. [source: https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/881784 | confidence: medium (sponsored) | 2025-06-20]
- FPT Japan's CEO said all its foreign employees hold N2 or above, helped by the company's own Japanese school. [source: https://vietcetera.com/vn/dieu-gi-dua-mot-doanh-nghiep-viet-vao-top-cong-ty-cong-nghe-hang-dau-nhat-ban | confidence: medium | about Oct 2024]

## (d) What this means for the practice plan (our inference, not fact)
- Prepare for 3 or more rounds: early rounds online, a final in person with executives, and possibly an aptitude test (type unknown).
- The most likely technical task is explaining the research simply in Japanese, to a non-specialist and to a division head (1-minute and 3-minute versions), plus evaluation metrics (mAP, precision/recall, FPS, metrics for time-series models).
- The automotive AI role is a bridge role. Practise in keigo: progress reports, explaining specs, handling customer requests, quality checks, and working with an offshore team in Vietnam.
- Prepare answers on working with Vietnamese and multinational colleagues, offshore development, attitude to English, possibly coding less, working at a demanding company, why FPT / why automotive / why Japan, and flexibility about location and client sites.
- Show One team, Challenge and Lifelong Learning in the ガクチカ and strength answers.
- Interviewers may not be native Japanese speakers, so practise listening to many styles of Japanese and answer in short, clear sentences.
- Timing: 28卒 tests and 2nd interviews were already being reported in Nov 2026. The AI/automotive internship during long holidays is a direct route.
